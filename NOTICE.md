# Data notices

No raw or processed data is redistributed. PrimeKG integrates multiple sources with individual licenses, including non-commercial DrugBank restrictions; its drug/disease descriptive fields are not used. MONDO and HPO are used for ontology labels, synonyms and hierarchy. MedlinePlus.gov and openFDA provide text; attribution is retained. BioRED is used only for extraction evaluation. BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 is downloaded by fastembed for local embeddings. Detailed source terms and caveats will accompany the final documentation.

This demo is not clinically validated and must not guide diagnosis, dosage or treatment.
